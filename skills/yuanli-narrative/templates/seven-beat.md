# 7-Beat Micro-loop Template

Apply this to each major narrative turn.

## 1｜Scene

Where are we? What concrete thing can the reader see, hear, or situate?

> 

## 2｜Tension

What is wrong, contradictory, costly, uncertain, or changing?

> 

## 3｜Choice

What did the subject actually choose to do or not do?

> 

## 4｜Mechanism

Why could that choice plausibly change the outcome?

Avoid labels like “vision” or “courage” unless the mechanism is explicit.

> 

## 5｜Consequence

What happened next? Separate immediate consequence from later Reality Settlement.

> 

## 6｜Compression

One memorable line that reinterprets the scene without overstating evidence:

> ** **

## 7｜Bridge

Open the next genuine question. Do not use empty transitions such as “但是故事还没有结束”.

> 

## Quality checks

- [ ] The scene contains at least one concrete element.
- [ ] Tension is real, not manufactured by adjectives.
- [ ] Choice is observable.
- [ ] Mechanism is explicit.
- [ ] Consequence is not confused with causality.
- [ ] Compression is earned by the scene.
- [ ] Bridge creates a new cognitive question.
