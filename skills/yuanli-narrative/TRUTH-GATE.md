# 原力叙事 2.0｜Truth Gate

Status: `CANDIDATE / YNS2-SK-A`

## Purpose

Truth Gate prevents a compelling narrative from silently upgrading weak evidence into strong causal claims.

A good story must be able to say “不知道”。可信度本身，就是叙事资产。

## Epistemic labels

### [F] Fact

Definition: supported by primary evidence, official records, audited data, original work, or multiple high-quality independent sources.

Allowed language: direct statement.

Example: “公司在 2007 年上市。”

### [R] Reported

Definition: a recollection, interview claim, biography anecdote, media report, or single-source account.

Allowed language: attribute the source.

Example: “按照某次采访中的回忆……”

Not allowed: silently convert the recollection into objective fact.

### [I] Inference

Definition: an interpretation derived from supported facts, but not itself directly stated by the subject or source.

Allowed language:
- “可以把它理解为……”
- “从现有证据看，更有解释力的可能是……”
- “这可能反映……”

### [H] Hypothesis

Definition: a competing causal explanation that remains open to falsification.

Required behavior:
- state at least one plausible alternative when causality is contested;
- allow the preferred explanation to lose;
- do not write hypothesis as destiny.

### [U] Unverified

Definition: rumor, unsourced anecdote, unclear attribution, unverifiable private motive, or claim without adequate evidence.

Rule: `[U]` may be omitted, quarantined, or explicitly named as unverified. It MUST NOT carry the main causal load of B05–B12.

## Claim ladder

Use the strongest language only at the strongest evidence level:

`[F] direct statement`

`[R] attributed statement`

`[I] interpretive bridge`

`[H] competing explanation`

`[U] quarantine / do not rely`

## Four hard rules

1. **越精彩的细节，越要查证。**
2. **不把传闻升级成事实。**
3. **不把观点伪装成史实。**
4. **不把人物神话成全部因果。**

## Causal claim gate

Before writing “因为 X，所以 Y”，check:

- Is X supported?
- Is Y supported?
- Is the X → Y mechanism evidenced or only inferred?
- Is there a competing explanation?
- Is Era Beta being mistaken for Person Alpha?
- Is hindsight rewriting an uncertain decision into an inevitable one?

If any answer is unclear, downgrade the language.

## Motive gate

Private motive is especially high-risk.

Never write:

> “他这样做就是因为……”

unless the motive has strong direct support.

Prefer:

> “现有证据能够确认的是动作本身；至于动机，更稳妥的解释有 A / B / C。”

## Hindsight gate

Do not turn later outcomes into proof that the subject predicted them.

Bad:

> “后来危机发生，证明他早就准确预测到了危机。”

Better:

> “后来危机发生，使当年的风险选择获得了新的现实结算；这不等于他当时精确预测了危机的时间和路径。”

## Collective-causality gate

For companies, films, scientific work, movements, and institutions:

- identify the focal person's high-leverage decisions;
- preserve independent contributions of partners, teams, institutions, capital, technology, regulation, and era;
- never collapse a collective outcome into one-person mythology.

## Publication checklist

Before final render:

- [ ] Every major causal claim has an epistemic level.
- [ ] `[U]` does not carry the main narrative.
- [ ] At least one competing explanation remains when appropriate.
- [ ] Hindsight has not been converted into foresight.
- [ ] Person Alpha and Era Beta are separated.
- [ ] Collective causality is preserved.
- [ ] Quotations are verified against the strongest available source.
- [ ] The conclusion is no more certain than the evidence permits.
